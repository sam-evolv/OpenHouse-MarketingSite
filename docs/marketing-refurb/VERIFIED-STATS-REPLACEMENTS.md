## Citation discipline for the live site

Every numerical claim on the live marketing pages must include a small grey footnote or caption with the source and date, in the same way the Law Society or RTB present their figures. Examples of the right style:

> *Source: Residential Tenancies Board, Q4 2024 Profile of the Register.*

> *Source: Central Statistics Office, New Dwelling Completions, 2025 full year.*

> *Source: SEAI National Retrofit Plan, Full Year Report 2024.*

This is non-negotiable. Citing the source is the credibility mechanism. Uncited stats look invented even when they're true.
